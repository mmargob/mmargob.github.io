Maxx Margob

Iranian-American researcher specializing in Iran policy, political analytics, and national security.

I am a recent graduate of the University of Maryland with a Master's in Applied Political Analytics and a Bachelor's in Government and Politics. My work sits at the intersection of quantitative social science and national security, with a particular focus on Iranian political behavior, authoritarian signaling, and U.S.-Iran relations.

As an Iranian American, I bring both technical expertise and firsthand cultural and political understanding to research on Iran and the broader Middle East. I combine natural language processing, statistical modeling, and political theory to generate insights about authoritarian regimes, electoral behavior, and security dynamics that are relevant to policymakers and analysts.
